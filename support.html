Common questions

I can’t sign in. On the sign-in screen tap “Forgot password” to get a reset link by email. Check your spam folder if it doesn’t arrive within a few minutes.

My check-in didn’t work. Check-ins need location access and you must be at the venue (within about 150 metres). Turn on location for BarChata in your phone’s settings and try again.

My Hearts are pending. Some venues confirm Hearts when staff scan your code or receipt. They appear once the venue confirms.

I’m not getting notifications. Allow notifications for BarChata in your phone’s settings, then open the app once so it can register your device.

How do I report or block someone? Tap the ••• menu on any post, story, Vibe, profile or message and choose Report or Block. Our team reviews every report. See our Community Guidelines.

How do I delete my account? In the app go to Me → Settings → Delete Account, or see barchata.com/delete-account.
