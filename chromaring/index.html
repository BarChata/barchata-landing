How it works

Four steps to full visibility.

From collar to dashboard in minutes. No rip-and-replace. No training weeks. No IT project.

Step 01

Tag your assets

Snap a Chroma-Ring collar or band onto each bottle, container, or pallet. Two seconds per item. Fluorescent markers are detected from any angle.

Step 02

Connect cameras

Point any IP camera at the work area. Bring your own — Reolink, Ubiquiti, Hikvision, or any brand with RTSP. Or add our kit cameras.

Step 03

Process at the edge

Our compact edge processor runs computer vision on-premises. All detection is local. Your video never leaves the building. Works offline too.

Step 04

Manage on dashboard

Live pour data, movement logs, anomaly alerts, and auto-reorder — all in the BarChata Biz dashboard or via API for enterprise integration.
